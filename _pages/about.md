Hi, I'm Huong Trang.

I work with data, build models, and try to make things work in practice.

With a background in credit risk modeling, I am now exploring Artificial Intelligence and how it can move beyond theory into real-world applications.

I’m interested in building practical systems, learning continuously, and turning ideas into something that actually works.

What I Do
------
Develop ML models (logistic regression, GBDT, explainability with SHAP)  
- Build automated pipelines with Python/R/SQL, Docker, and monitoring systems  
- Work with structured/tabular data at scale  
- Run experiments, evaluate models, and design reliable data workflows  

These experiences shaped my strengths in modeling, MLOps foundations, and systematic thinking — all of which translate well into biomedical AI.

What I Care About
------
I’m deeply interested in how AI can help with:

- Understanding biosignals (ECG, EEG, PPG, EMG)  
- Modeling clinical tabular data for decision support  
- Raman spectroscopy & spectral ML for early cancer detection  
- Small-data learning — a core challenge in biomedical domains  
- Model transparency to make AI safer in healthcare  

I care about building models that clinicians can trust, interpret, and use confidently.

What I’m Exploring Now
------
- Biosignal ML pipelines & feature extraction  
- Diffusion and generative models for biomedical data  
- Interpretable ML techniques (SHAP, attention mechanisms)  
- Benchmarking small clinical datasets  
- Reproducible research workflows  

I'm also developing projects for my BioAI research portfolio.

What I Aim to Do Next
------
My goal is to pursue a **Master’s in AI/ML** with a specialization in healthcare or bioinformatics in Europe.  
I want to work on problems involving:

- early disease detection  
- signal-level understanding of physiological data  
- AI systems that augment diagnosis and improve patient outcomes  

In the long run, I want to contribute to responsible, human-centered AI in medicine.

**Beyond Work:**
I enjoy learning languages, creative AI projects, and documenting my learning journey through writing and small experiments.
